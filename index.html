Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 13, 2026

TwistTalk is a tongue twister practice app. We built it to be simple and respectful of your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

TwistTalk collects only what it needs to function:

  • Audio recordings — When you practice a tongue twister, your voice is recorded temporarily to evaluate your pronunciation. Audio is sent to Google's Gemini API for transcription and scoring, then discarded. We do not store your audio on our servers.
  • Practice scores and history — Your scores and session history are stored locally on your device using an on-device database (SQLite). This data does not leave your device unless you choose to share it.
  • Device information — We may collect basic device information (operating system version, app version) to improve app stability. No personally identifiable information is collected.

How We Use Your Information

  • To score your tongue twister pronunciation using AI transcription
  • To display your practice history and leaderboard scores within the app
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

Third-Party Services

TwistTalk uses the following third-party service:

  • Google Gemini API — Used for audio transcription and pronunciation scoring. Audio data is sent to Google's servers for processing. Google's privacy policy applies to this data: policies.google.com/privacy

Data Storage and Security

Your practice data (scores, history) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. Audio recordings are processed in real time and are not permanently stored by us.

Children's Privacy

TwistTalk is a general-audience app suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your Rights

Since your practice data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling TwistTalk. You have full control over your local data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the effective date above. Continued use of TwistTalk after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
